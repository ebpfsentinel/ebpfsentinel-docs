# Your Data

> **Edition: Enterprise**

What the portal holds about you is yours to take away and yours to have deleted,
and what your agents sent is yours to erase. None of it needs anybody at our
end. The first two are on **Your account**, under **Take a copy of your data, or
delete your account**; the third is on **Analytics** inside the organisation.

## Take a copy

**Download the file** saves one file with your account, the organisations you
belong to and what you have done in the portal. No password is asked for: it
holds nothing you cannot already read on the screens.

It does not include what your organisations hold. A subscription, a licence or a
colleague's activity belongs to the organisation rather than to you.

## Delete your account

**Delete this account**, then **Delete it**. Past fifteen minutes since you
signed in or last confirmed, it asks for your password again first, see
[When the portal asks for your password again](signing-in.md#when-the-portal-asks-for-your-password-again).
It cannot be undone: your name,
your address and everything that identifies you are taken, you are signed out of
every browser, and any organisation you are the last member of closes with you.

It is refused while:

- you are the last owner of an organisation that has other members: make
  somebody else an owner, or remove the other members, from **Members**;
- a subscription is still open in one of your organisations: end it first, from
  **Billing**, see [Billing and Licence Keys](billing-and-licence-keys.md).

The screen names the organisation and links to the screen that clears the
obstacle.

Two clocks apply after a deletion. The technical and audit trace is kept thirty
days. Anything that is an accounting record, such as what a subscription was paid,
is kept ten years, because commercial law requires it.

## Erase what your agents sent

Telemetry is off until somebody turns it on for a deployment, see
[Optionally, send counters](../getting-started/from-purchase-to-reporting-agents.md#7-optionally-send-counters).
To remove what was already sent, open **Analytics**, then **Erase what was
sent**:

1. Press **Erase readings** and choose under **What to erase** every deployment
   or one deployment, including one already retired.
2. Press **Erase them**. Past fifteen minutes since you signed in or last
   confirmed, it asks for your password again first.

The answer arrives once the readings are actually gone. What each period counted
against your allowance is kept: it carries no name, and it is our own record of
what was metered.

Erasing does not turn telemetry off: a deployment that is sending keeps sending.
Turning it off is done on the deployment's **Telemetry** screen with **Turn
telemetry off**, which also revokes every ingest token of that deployment and
leaves what was already sent in place until you erase it.
