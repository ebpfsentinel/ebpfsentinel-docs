# Support Tickets

> **Edition: any plan, including open source**

Support is written. You open a ticket in the portal, somebody at our end answers
it in writing, and the whole exchange stays on the ticket. There is no call to
book and nothing to wait for on the phone. How quickly the first answer arrives
depends on your plan, and the [pricing page](https://ebpfsentinel.io/pricing)
states each target.

## Opening a ticket

**Support**, inside the organisation, lists the organisation's tickets and opens
new ones. Every member can open a ticket, read the answers and close it: a
member without an administrative role is exactly who this is for. The address
`portal.ebpfsentinel.io/support` takes you there directly, to the organisation
the portal opens on for you, after signing in if you were not.

A ticket takes a subject line, a message and, if the question is about one,
the deployment it concerns. Write what you did, what you expected and what you
saw instead, and leave out secrets and personal data.

## What the ticket carries for you

When the ticket opens, the portal copies two things into it so that nobody has
to ask you for them:

- the plans in force on the organisation;
- the agent versions your machines in service report, narrowed to the chosen
  deployment's machines when you name one.

They are copied as they stood at that moment and are shown on the ticket.
Nothing else is attached on your behalf: no log, no configuration, no file, no
address. Besides those two lines, we read only what you write.

## Answers and closing

A ticket is **Waiting on us** until we answer, **Answered** once we have, and
**Closed** when somebody in your organisation closes it. Replying to an answered
ticket puts it back with us. Only your side closes a ticket, and a closed
ticket takes no more messages: if the problem comes back, open a new one.

When an answer arrives, the person who opened the ticket receives a mail with a
link to it, while they are still a member. The mail does not carry the answer
itself; you read it in the portal.

Opening, replying to and closing a ticket each write a line in the
organisation's [audit log](audit-log.md).
