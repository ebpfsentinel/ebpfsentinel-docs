# Mail From Us

> **Edition: any plan, including open source**

Apart from the mail your own actions cause (a sign-in link, a receipt, an
invitation), we write two kinds of message to the people of an organisation.
Both leave from the portal, through the same mail service as every other
account message. Nobody at our end who writes one ever sees your address: the
portal looks each address up at the moment it sends, from your account as it
stands then.

## Service messages

A service message is about what you pay for: a change of price or terms, an
incident, a release found faulty that your machines still run, or something
you have to act on. Its first line says why you receive it. It reaches the
people whose role in your organisation it concerns.

Service messages always arrive and carry no way to turn them off, because they
carry what the contract obliges us to tell you.

If a managed service provider runs your organisation, the owners of that
provider receive a copy of every service message sent to you, so they can act
on it for you.

## Product news

Product news is what we released and what is coming. It is the one kind of
mail you choose, organisation by organisation:

| Your organisation | Product news |
|---|---|
| Buys as a business | On until you turn it off |
| Buys as a consumer | Off until you turn it on |
| Is run by a managed service provider | Never sent: your provider writes to you instead |

To change it, open **Your account**, then **Mail**: one switch per organisation
you belong to, taking effect on the next message.

Every product news message also carries a link to stop it. The link opens a
page with one button, **Stop product news**, and needs no sign-in. It stops
news through the organisation that message was sent for, and nothing else:
service messages keep arriving.

## What is kept, and for how long

For each message we keep what was written, who at our end wrote it and why,
and which organisations and roles it was addressed to. For each person it was
sent to we keep one line: whether it was delivered and what the receiving mail
server answered. That line names your account, never your address, and stops
naming it when the account is deleted.

Each person's line is deleted 12 months after the send, and the message itself
after 24 months. The [privacy notice](https://ebpfsentinel.io/legal/privacy)
states the same.
